FitLog · Account deletion

Delete your FitLog account and data

FitLog (by Walid El bahloul) lets you permanently delete your account and the data linked to it, directly inside the app, at any time.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the FitLog app and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger zone section.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm by tapping Yes, delete everything.

The deletion happens right away and cannot be undone.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your profile (email, age, sex, height, activity level, goal)Permanently deleted
Your weight history and macro targetsPermanently deleted
Your food logs and your corrections to AI estimatesPermanently deleted
Backups containing your dataPurged on our standard rotation, typically within 30 days
Entries in the shared food catalog (food names and nutrition values, no personal information)Kept, because they are not linked to you

If you can't open the app

Email walydalbahloul@gmail.com from the address you used to register, with the subject "Delete my FitLog account". We will delete your account and its data and confirm by email, no later than 30 days after your request.

Meals logged without an account

If you used FitLog without creating an account, your meals are stored only on your device. Delete them by removing the app, or by deleting a meal from the Diary screen.
